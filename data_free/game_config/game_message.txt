Welcome, Keeper. A few things about playing in a shared world.

PROTECTION
You are protected from other keepers for 48000 turns, or 5 days of
real time, whichever runs out first. Once either one expires the
protection is gone and your dungeon can be invaded.

REINVASION
After someone invades you, you are protected for 15 minutes before
anyone can invade you again.

ACCOUNT LOCKOUT
If you crash, or close the game with the X instead of saving and
exiting, your account stays locked for 5 minutes. You will not be
able to log back in until it clears.

CHEATS
Your accounts should have in the left menu a way to spawn a minion
of your choosing or an item in quantities. Use them however you want
